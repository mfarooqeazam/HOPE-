Our vision

Therapy is not a luxury. It is only priced like one.

A month of therapy at the cheapest rate in Islamabad costs four-fifths of a full minimum-wage income. At the intensity many children actually need, it costs about twice that income — and the household still has rent, food and any other children.

PKR 30,000 A month at the cheapest end — five sessions a week at PKR 1,500. 81% of a full minimum-wage income
PKR 72,000 A month at six sessions a week at PKR 3,000 — common for high support needs, not extreme. 195% — nearly twice that income
PKR 37,000 The monthly minimum wage in Islamabad — the whole household income, for many families. ICT, financial year 2025–26

That is the gap this centre exists to close. Four things are given away — therapy, camps, advice and schooling — funded by paid training, fee-paying families, and donations.

Photography — licence and honesty

Every stand-in photograph is a real, free-licence Unsplash photo, credited in the corner of its frame (hover it). Unsplash's licence permits commercial use without attribution — the credits are there because a centre that talks about consent should show its sources anyway. The three founder portraits are the exception: they are the client's own photograph, so they carry no credit.

Two things these photos are not. They are not AI-generated — I have no image generation available, so nothing here is synthetic. And they are not your centre. Your own CLAUDE.md says never stock images, and it is right: these are comps that show the layout breathing, to be swapped for photographs of your actual rooms, staff and materials. Drag any image onto any frame and it replaces the stand-in and persists.

The founder portrait is the one real photograph of the centre in these comps — Muhammad Farooq E Azam Awan, supplied by the client and now placed in all three options. It is also the template for the rest: the credential block only works when the face above it is genuinely the person the certificate names.
