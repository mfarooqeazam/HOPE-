Where The Project Hope works

The centre is in Islamabad. The certification behind it is not — an IBAO credential is portable across borders, which is what makes training and consultation possible well beyond Pakistan.

Where these numbers come from. The 108 countries shown in gold are every country in the IBAO’s own public certification directory, read from its country filter — these are places where IBAO certification is active, which is what makes training delivered here count towards a recognised credential. The eight in sage are the largest Pakistani communities abroad by published census figures, and are where remote consultation has a real audience rather than a claim we already serve them. Countries too small to draw at this scale are marked with a dot. Confirm the remote-consultation list, and ask IBAO in writing for the countries an Approved Content Provider may deliver in — nothing IBAO publishes matches the figure of 56.

Our vision

Help should not depend on what a family can pay

A diagnosis is expensive enough before the therapy starts. The Project Hope gives four things away, and asks nothing for them — funded by our paid training programmes, by fee-paying therapy families, and by donations.

Funded therapy places

ABA, speech, occupational therapy and physiotherapy. A number of places are funded rather than charged for, awarded on need — therapy or schooling, whichever a child needs.

Free camps

Open sessions for children and families, run by the centre’s therapists at no charge. What the camps are, how often they run, and who they are for.

Free advice & consultation

The first conversation costs nothing. Ask what you are looking at, what to do next, and where to go — even if that turns out not to be us.

Funded school places

An inclusive classroom where neurodivergent and neurotypical children learn side by side, with a number of places funded rather than charged for.

Every one of these is paid for by someone. A donation buys a therapy hour, a school place, or the materials a classroom runs on. what a specific amount buys — e.g. “PKR X funds one child’s schooling for a month”
