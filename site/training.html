IBA & IBT certification

Behaviour analysis training delivered in Islamabad as an IBAO Approved Content Provider, with mentorship, supervision and continuing education units from a certified supervisor.

Pathways

Two routes, depending on where you are

IBT — International Behavior Technician

40 hours. The entry route for people beginning in behaviour analysis: teaching assistants, shadow teachers, therapy assistants, and parents who want a formal grounding.

  • Foundations of behaviour analysis, measurement and ethics
  • Supervised practice with real feedback

schedule, format (in person/online), prerequisites, assessment, fee, next intake date

IBA — International Behavior Analyst

270 hours. The full professional certification, for those intending to practise as behaviour analysts and to supervise others.

  • Assessment, intervention design, and data-based decision making
  • Ethics, supervision and cultural competency
  • Mentorship from a certified IBA mentor
