The portal

One place to start, whoever you are. Families looking for therapy or a school place, professionals enrolling on the IBA or IBT pathways, and volunteers offering time — all three routes begin here.

Three routes

Which one are you?

For families

Therapy or a school place

Applied Behavior Analysis (ABA), speech and language therapy, occupational therapy, physiotherapy, educational support, or a place at the inclusive school.

What you will need

  • Your name and a phone number we can reach you on
  • Your child’s first name and age
  • A sentence about what you are looking for, in your own words

Not needed: a diagnosis, a referral, or any medical records. Please do not send reports through this site — bring them to the appointment.

Register as a family

For professionals

IBA & IBT training

The 270-hour IBA and 40-hour IBT pathways, delivered by an IBAO Approved Content Provider, with supervision, mentorship and continuing education units.

What you will need

  • Your name, phone number and email
  • Which pathway you are asking about
  • Your current role and any relevant experience

Worth knowing: hours signed here are hours the IBAO accepts. Ask about the next intake before you commit to anything.

Enrol or ask about training

For volunteers

Give time or skills

Camps, the classroom, adult and older-adult services, admin and fundraising, a professional skill, or help from wherever you are.

What you will need

  • Your name and a phone number
  • Whether you are an individual, a student or professional, a company, or remote
  • Roughly when you are free

Before you start: minimum age 16, or 18 for adult services. Screening and a half-day induction come first, and both are free.

A note on accounts

There is no login, and that is deliberate

A portal usually means an account, a password, and a dashboard holding your child’s records. We have not built that, because holding clinical records online creates a duty we are not yet ready to discharge properly — a retention period, a deletion route, and a breach procedure, all in writing.

So this site collects a name, a way to reach you, and whatever you choose to tell us in your own words. It does not ask for a diagnosis or medical history, and it never will through a web form. Progress, plans and reports are discussed with you directly.

If accounts would genuinely help — session notes, progress you can read between visits, scheduling — say so, and we will build it properly rather than quickly.
