# Placeholder photography — REPLACE BEFORE LAUNCH


These are **placeholders**, not photographs of this centre. Every frame carries
a visible `placeholder` badge, and the rule stands: nothing may imply these are
HOPE's own rooms, staff or children.

Most are the design handoff's Unsplash comps. Six come from Wikimedia Commons
or Flickr, because the comp that shipped in that frame showed the wrong thing.

## What was wrong, and what changed

Three frames — speech and language, IBA supervision, and volunteering — all
carried photographs from the same shoot of a client lying on a couch. That is
the psychoanalysis trope, and it is wrong three times over: speech therapy is
an exchange across a table, IBA supervision is practitioners discussing a case,
and volunteering is not a clinical session at all. An inclusive classroom was
illustrated by a village group in Santa hats, and "the building" by a domed
shopping arcade.

Most frames were fixed by **re-mapping** rather than replacing: the photograph
was fine, it was in the wrong slot. The therapy-mat session moved to "a session
in progress"; the colour-chart conversation moved to "the first conversation".
Six frames had nothing suitable in the pool and were sourced fresh.

Every photograph now appears exactly once — checked by file hash, not by eye.

## Licensing — read this before launch

The Unsplash comps carry the Unsplash licence: commercial use permitted, no
attribution required. The six Creative Commons files are marked below, and the
`by` / `by-sa` ones **require visible attribution wherever they are
published**. This file satisfies that in the repository, not on a deployed
site. Before launch either replace them with the centre's own photographs —
the intended outcome — or publish a credits page carrying the rows below.

`by-sa` additionally means a cropped version stays `by-sa`. Each file here was
resized and centre-cropped to fit its frame, so that condition is live.

## How to replace one

1. Drop your photograph here with the **same filename**.
2. Make a 700px-wide copy named `<name>-sm.webp` (the `srcset` fallback).
3. Keep the same pixel dimensions, or update `width`/`height` in the markup —
   they are what holds cumulative layout shift at zero.
4. When every frame in a section is real, drop `frame--comp` from that markup
   to remove the badge, and delete that row here.
