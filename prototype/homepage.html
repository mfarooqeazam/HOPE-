About

Where it all began

the founding story, in your own words — what you saw that made you start this

Muhammad Farooq E Azam Awan is an internationally certified Behavior Analyst and clinical psychology practitioner. He founded The Project hope in August 2025 as a multidisciplinary centre for children with special needs, and leads its clinical teams, its training programmes, and its free inclusive school.

His practice draws on Applied Behaviour Analysis alongside CBT, REBT and psychodynamic approaches, with functional behaviour assessment and data-driven intervention planning at its core. Before founding the centre he worked as an Assistant Applied Behavior Analyst at Shifa Integrated HealthCare Technologies, as a Special Educational Needs teacher at Beaconhouse School System, and as an ABA and psychology intern at Shifa International Hospital.

Education

  • MS Clinical Psychology — SZABIST, Islamabad. Completed 2026. Thesis: Family Involvement in ABA and Skill Generalization in Children: The Moderating Role of Caregiver Stress in Nuclear Families.
  • BS Psychology — COMSATS University Islamabad. Graduated February 2024.
  • Continuing education — Ethics, Supervision & Cultural Competency (6 CEUs, IBAO); Clinical Care for Autistic Adults; AFIRM series, 31 evidence-based autism intervention modules.
